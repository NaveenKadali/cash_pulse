## 1. Purpose & Background

Independent wedding and event photographers are often financially fragile. Client balances arrive late (especially the final 30% after photo selection and album approval), while subcontracted crew, drone pilots, print labs, and software subscriptions are due on fixed dates. The result is cash shortfalls even when the business is profitable.

CashPulse gives a photography studio owner one consolidated view of:
- who owes the studio money and how late it is,
- which vendor bills are due and when,
- what each project actually leaves the studio after costs,
- whether there will be enough cash for the next 30–90 days.

**Guiding question for every feature:** *Does this help the studio owner see who owes what, what vendors are due, and whether they will have cash next month?* Features that do not answer this question are out of scope.

## 2. Goals & Non-Goals

### 2.1 Goals (v1)
- G1. Record every project's contract value, payment milestones, and payments received.
- G2. Show outstanding client balances with aging, and support follow-up messages.
- G3. Record vendor bills and their due dates.
- G4. Show per-project profit after outsourced costs.
- G5. Forecast daily cash position for the next 30–90 days and flag shortfalls.
- G6. Give the client a simple, secure page showing payments, balance due, and project progress, with a UPI payment action.
- G7. Produce a daily morning brief with prioritised actions.

### 2.2 Non-Goals (v1)
- NG1. Not a general accounting or bookkeeping system (no ledger, chart of accounts, double-entry books, or statutory reports).
- NG2. No GST or other tax calculation. All amounts are flat, net, final figures entered by the user.
- NG3. No payment gateway in production. A gateway test-mode adapter (Cashfree or Razorpay test) may exist only for evaluation and sandbox use and never moves real money. No fees, KYC, or webhooks in production.
- NG4. No bank or UPI statement import, no Account Aggregator integration, no automatic reconciliation. Payments are confirmed manually.
- NG5. No photo hosting, photo proofing, or image preview gallery. Galleries stay on the studio's existing tools (e.g. Google Drive).
- NG6. Does not manage album design files, printing production, or vendor-side work. CashPulse targets the photography business owner, not designers or print labs.
- NG7. No client login accounts. Clients access only through the shared client page link.

## 3. Users & Roles

### 3.1 Personas
- **Primary – Studio Owner / Lead Photographer** (e.g. a solo wedding studio doing about 22 weddings and 15 pre-weddings/events a year, average ticket ₹80,000–₹1,80,000). Works from WhatsApp, Google Drive, a paper notebook, and a UPI app. Needs fast answers about cash and collections.
- **Secondary – Team Member** (associate photographer, editor, or assistant on a team of 2–5 people). Needs to update project progress, not see money.
- **Stakeholder – End Client** (couple or family). Wants clear milestones, a simple way to pay, and no pressure. Uses the client page only.
- **Stakeholder – Vendors** (freelance second shooters, drone pilots, print labs, designers). Not users of CashPulse. Their bills are recorded by the studio.

## 4. Key Terms

- **Client:** the person or family who books the studio.
- **Project:** one booked event (e.g. a wedding) for one client, with a contract value, a payment schedule, costs, and a progress stage.
- **Milestone:** a scheduled part of the contract value. The default template is 30% advance, 40% at shoot, 30% final balance; this is only a default. Milestones are flexible per project (FR-A-08). Each milestone has an amount, an expected or due date, and a status.
- **Payment:** money received from a client against one or more milestones. Recorded manually with amount, date, method (UPI, cash, bank transfer, other), and transaction reference.
- **Vendor:** an outsourced party (second shooter, drone pilot, editor, album designer, print lab, software tool).
- **Vendor Bill:** an amount owed to a vendor, linked to a project or to general overhead, with a due date and a status (unpaid, partially paid, paid). Status is derived from recorded Vendor Payments.
- **Vendor Payment:** money the studio pays to a vendor against a Vendor Bill, possibly in several parts (FR-B-07).
- **Recurring Expense:** a regular outflow (e.g. SaaS subscription, EMI) with a day-of-month due date.
- **Opening Cash Balance:** the studio's bank/UPI balance as entered by the owner on a given date. Used as the starting point for the forecast.
- **Deliverable Stage:** the progress of a project through the stages listed in FR-E-01.
- **Financial Status:** the money position of a project, separate from the Deliverable Stage. Values: Financially Open, Partially Settled, Financially Closed (FR-E-04).
- **Expected project contribution:** the amount a project is expected to leave the studio after outsourced costs (FR-C-01, §7 rule 5).
- **Client Page:** the shared, read-only settlement and progress page for one project (FR-G).

## 5. User Stories

### Studio Owner
- US-01: As an owner, I want to create a project with the client's name, event date, and contract value, so that every booking is tracked in one place.
- US-02: As an owner, I want the contract value split into milestones automatically (default 30/40/30, editable), so that I don't calculate invoices by hand.
- US-03: As an owner, I want to record a payment against a milestone with its reference, so that the balance updates immediately.
- US-04: As an owner, I want to see which client balances are overdue and by how many days, so that I know whom to contact first.
- US-05: As an owner, I want a ready-to-send follow-up message for a client, so that I can ask for money without damaging the relationship.
- US-06: As an owner, I want to record vendor costs against a project with their due dates, so that I know what each project really earns.
- US-07: As an owner, I want to see my projected cash balance day by day for the next 30–90 days, so that I know if I will run short.
- US-08: As an owner, I want to see which overdue client payment is causing a future vendor payment to be at risk, so that I know which call to make first.
- US-09: As an owner, I want a morning brief listing today's priority actions, so that I start the day knowing what to do.
- US-10: As an owner, I want to share a client page with a couple, so that they can see what is paid, what is due, and pay by UPI.
- US-11: As an owner, I want to invite team members and give them a role, so that they can update projects without seeing my money.
- US-12: As an owner, I want to see how much a project actually keeps after vendor costs, so that I can price future packages better.

### Team Member
- US-13: As a team member, I want to update a project's stage (e.g. "Shoot Completed", "Proofs Ready"), so that the client page and the owner see current progress.
- US-14: As a team member, I want to see the schedule of my projects (event dates and stages), so that I can plan my work.
- US-15: As a team member, I must not see bank balances, studio profit, or receivable and payable totals, so that the studio's finances stay private.

### End Client
- US-16: As a client, I want to see what I have already paid and what is still due, with a due date, so that I know exactly where I stand.
- US-17: As a client, I want to pay the due amount by scanning a UPI QR code or tapping a button that opens my UPI app, so that paying is quick.
- US-18: As a client, I want to see how far my project has progressed, so that I know when my album and deliveries are coming.

## 6. Functional Requirements

Priority: **P0** = must have for v1; **P1** = should have for v1; **P2** = later.

### A. Customer & Payment Management

- **FR-A-01 (P0): Client and project records.** The system shall store clients and projects. A project has a client, event date(s), contract value (flat, net, in ₹), status, and an optional notes field.
- **FR-A-02 (P0): Milestone schedule.** When a project is created, the system shall create three milestones from the contract value using the default template 30%, 40%, and 30%. The default is a starting point only; the owner can edit percentages, amounts, and due dates (rules in FR-A-08). Milestone amounts must sum to the contract value; the system must block saving if they do not.
- **FR-A-03 (P0): Record payments.** The owner shall record a payment with: milestone, amount, date received, method, and transaction reference (required for UPI and bank transfer). A payment can cover part of a milestone, and one payment can be split across several milestones (see FR-A-08). Overpayment shall be blocked or flagged for explicit confirmation.
- **FR-A-04 (P0): Balance tracking.** The system shall show for each milestone and each project: amount due, amount received, and remaining balance.
- **FR-A-05 (P0): Receivables aging.** The system shall classify each unpaid milestone by days past due into buckets: not yet due, 1–15 days, 16–30 days, 31–60 days, 61+ days. Totals per bucket are shown to the owner only.
- **FR-A-06 (P1): Follow-up messages.** For any overdue milestone, the owner can generate one of three message tones (see FR-F-01) pre-filled with client name, invoice/milestone reference, amount, and client page or UPI link. The owner copies or opens the message and sends it from their own WhatsApp. CashPulse does not send messages itself in v1.
- **FR-A-07 (P1): Correct, void, and payment history.** Each project shows its full payment history. A recorded payment can be edited or voided by the owner; every change is kept in a visible correction trail (who, when, old and new value, reason).
- **FR-A-08 (P0): Flexible milestones.** The 30/40/30 split is only the default template. The owner can change milestones under these rules:
  - An amount change after a partial payment is allowed if the milestones still total the contract value; prior payments are kept against their milestones.
  - A due-date change after a milestone is overdue is allowed and logged.
  - One payment can be split across several milestones; each share is recorded separately.
  - A contract value change is allowed with a logged reason.
- **FR-A-09 (P1): Cancellation and refunds.** A project can be cancelled; its payment and milestone history is kept. A refund is recorded as a negative payment with a reason, and it is shown in the payment history and correction trail.

### B. Outsourced Vendor Expense Management

- **FR-B-01 (P0): Vendor directory.** The owner shall maintain vendors with name, category (second shooter, drone pilot, editor, album designer, print lab, software/tool, other), contact details, and default rate (optional).
- **FR-B-02 (P0): Vendor bills.** The owner shall record a vendor bill with vendor, amount, due date, optional project link, and status (unpaid, partially paid, or paid). Status is derived from recorded Vendor Payments (FR-B-07).
- **FR-B-03 (P0): Estimated vs actual costs.** A project can hold estimated costs (before the event) and actual bills (as they arrive). The project view shows both.
- **FR-B-04 (P0): Recurring expenses (basic).** The owner can define a recurring expense with name, amount, day of month, and start and end dates. It appears automatically in each month of the forecast within its start and end dates. Advanced recurrence rules are P1 and are not part of this requirement.
- **FR-B-05 (P0): Payables aging.** The system shall list unpaid and partially paid vendor bills sorted by due date, showing the remaining payable for each, with overdue items highlighted.
- **FR-B-06 (P1): Team payout rates are restricted.** Payout rates for team members and freelance crew are visible only to the owner (see §3.2).
- **FR-B-07 (P0): Partial vendor payments.** The owner can record multiple payments against one vendor bill. Each payment has amount, date, method, and reference. The system shows the remaining payable and the full payment history for the bill.

### C. Project-Level Financial Visibility

- **FR-C-01 (P0): Project financial summary.** For each project the system shows three figures:
  - **Expected project contribution** = contract value − (actual costs, or estimated costs where actuals are not yet recorded)
  - **Actual cash collected** = sum of payments received on the project
  - **Outstanding project costs** = remaining payable on linked vendor bills
  
  Also shown: total customer amount (contract value), remaining customer balance, and retention percentage = expected project contribution ÷ contract value.
  
  **Rule:** an estimate is replaced by the actual bill once it is recorded, and is never counted twice.
- **FR-C-02 (P0): Project list.** A list of all projects with stage, event date, balance remaining, and retention percentage. The owner sees all columns; a Team Member sees only stage and event date.
- **FR-C-03 (P1): Studio profit summary.** The owner sees total retained profit across projects for a selectable period. Hidden from Team Members.

### D. Cash-Flow Forecasting

- **FR-D-01 (P0): Opening balance.** The owner enters the studio's cash balance (bank + UPI) and its date. Forecast starts from this value. The owner can update it at any time.
- **FR-D-02 (P0): Inflow schedule.** Forecast inflows are unpaid milestone balances placed on their expected date. Milestones with no date use the project's event date plus a default lag (configurable; default 30 days for the final balance). Each inflow is marked with its confidence: *scheduled* (date set by owner) or *estimated* (system-assumed date).
- **FR-D-03 (P0): Outflow schedule.** Forecast outflows are remaining payables on unpaid and partially paid vendor bills on their due dates, plus recurring expenses on their days.
- **FR-D-04 (P0): Daily runway.** The system computes a day-by-day projected balance for the chosen horizon (30, 60, or 90 days): opening balance + cumulative inflows − cumulative outflows.
- **FR-D-05 (P0): Shortfall flag.** Any day where the projected balance falls below zero (or below a configurable safety threshold) is flagged, with the date, the shortfall amount, and the outflows causing it.
- **FR-D-06 (P0): Collision attribution.** For each shortfall, the system lists the inflows that could help cover it (e.g. "Verma family balance ₹45,000 could help cover the Friday lab bill shortfall of ₹70,000"). The system never states that an inflow "will resolve" a shortfall. This is a deterministic calculation (see §7 rule 9), not generated text.
- **FR-D-07 (P0): What-if scenarios.** The owner can delay key inflows by 0–30 days and see the runway and shortfall dates update. The original forecast is kept for comparison.
- **FR-D-08 (P0): Forecast consistency with recorded money.** The forecast must not double-count money already recorded:
  - Recorded client payments are removed from future inflows.
  - Recorded vendor payments are removed from outflows; only the remaining payable stays in outflows.
  - Transactions dated on or before the opening balance date are not counted again.
  - Each inflow and outflow is marked *owner-confirmed* or *system-estimated*, and estimated items show their assumptions (e.g. default lag used).
- **FR-D-09 (P0): Lowest projected balance.** The forecast shows the lowest projected balance within the horizon and the date on which it occurs.

### E. Project Progress (Deliverables) & Financial Status

- **FR-E-01 (P0): Progress stage per project.** Each project has one current stage in this fixed sequence:
  1. Booking
  2. Vendor Setup
  3. Shoot Completed
  4. Digital Proofs Ready
  5. Album Layout Approved
  6. Print & Delivered
  7. Financial Closed
  - Stage 7 is derived automatically from the financial status in FR-E-04 and is never set manually.
- **FR-E-02 (P0): Stage updates.** Owner and Team Members can move a project to the next stage (or back by one, with a note). Each change is logged with user and date.
- **FR-E-03 (P0): Stage and money linkage (display).** The client page and owner views show when album dispatch and high-resolution downloads are on hold because a milestone is unpaid. The release itself is performed by the studio outside CashPulse; CashPulse displays the status only. The hold rule is configurable per project (on or off).
- **FR-E-04 (P0): Financial status.** Each project has a financial status, kept separate from its deliverable stage (FR-E-01). The values are:
  - *Financially Open*: client balance or linked vendor payables remain.
  - *Partially Settled*: some client or vendor money has been settled, but not all.
  - *Financially Closed*: the project is closed only when the client balance is zero and all linked vendor bills are fully paid.

### F. Assisted Multi-Tone Follow-Ups, Dashboard & AI Insights

- **FR-F-01 (P0): Three deterministic message templates.** Always available, with no AI required:
  - **Tone 1 – Celebratory sneak-peek:** friendly thank-you with the project's client page link and a request to shortlist photos.
  - **Tone 2 – Album approved / print release gate:** states the layout is final, names the remaining milestone amount, and gives the UPI link.
  - **Tone 3 – Firm due notice:** names the invoice/milestone, amount, and days overdue; states that dispatch and downloads remain on hold until settled; gives the UPI link.
- **FR-F-02 (P0): AI-drafted nudge wording.** The owner can request a rewritten version of a template in a different warmth level or language. The AI (via AiServiceAdapter, Google Gemini) receives only the facts already computed by the system (names, amounts, dates). The owner reviews the draft before copying it. If GEMINI_API_KEY is missing, or the call fails or times out, the deterministic template in FR-F-01 is shown instead.
- **FR-F-03 (P0): Morning brief.** Once per day (on opening the app, or at a time the owner sets), the system shows a short prioritised list of actions. The ordering and the figures come from deterministic calculations. Wording may be produced by the AI assistant (Google Gemini, gemini-1.5-flash, via AiServiceAdapter). The deterministic fallback is used when GEMINI_API_KEY is missing, when a call fails, or when it times out.
- **FR-F-04 (P0): Alerts.** The system raises in-app alerts for: a milestone becoming overdue, a vendor bill due within 3 days, a forecast shortfall within the horizon, and a stage not updated for a configurable number of days. Alerts are visible to the owner; Team Members see only alerts about their own projects' stages.
- **FR-F-05 (P0): Fact-only AI.** Any AI-generated text must cite or be built from the deterministic figures supplied to it. No AI output is used to calculate balances, aging, or runway.
- **FR-F-06 (P0): Dashboard totals.** The owner dashboard shows: total receivables, total payables, available cash, lowest projected balance, and the date of that lowest balance. Team Members do not see these totals (§3.2).
- **FR-F-07 (P1): De-duplicated alerts.** The same underlying issue produces one alert, not repeated alerts on each screen or each day, until its status changes.

### G. Client Page (`/p/:token`)

- **FR-G-01 (P0): Unique, unguessable link.** Each project has a client page at a path using a random token (proposed: at least 128 bits of randomness). A readable name may appear on the page, but the token alone grants access. The owner can regenerate or revoke a link at any time, and optionally set an expiry date.
- **FR-G-02 (P0): Page content.** The page shows, read-only:
  - Project name and event date
  - Total package amount
  - Amount paid, with the date of each payment (no transaction internals beyond date, amount, and method)
  - Pending balance, the milestone it belongs to, and its due date
  - Deliverables stepper (the seven stages in FR-E-01, with the current one highlighted)
  - Hold notice when FR-E-03 applies
  - Studio name and contact details
- **FR-G-03 (P0): UPI payment action.** For the pending amount, the page shows:
  - A dynamic UPI QR code, and
  - A button that opens the client's UPI app with the amount, payee (studio UPI ID and name), and a transaction note (e.g. "Invoice 108 – Kavita & Rohan").
  
  The UPI link format is: `upi://pay?pa=<studio-upi-id>&pn=<studio-name>&am=<amount>&tn=<note>&cu=INR`. All parameter values must be safely URL-encoded, and the UPI flow must be tested on Android and iOS. Opening a UPI app does not confirm payment. In production, UPI deep-link and dynamic QR are the only payment methods. A "Simulate UPI Payment" action exists only in evaluation/sandbox mode, is labelled as a simulation, and never creates a real payment.
- **FR-G-04 (P0): Payment submission and confirmation.** The client page does not confirm payment. After paying, the client may tap "I have paid" and optionally enter a UPI or bank reference (UTR). The owner receives a "Payment submitted – verify & settle" alert and must verify and record the payment manually (FR-A-03). The page shows "Payment pending confirmation" until the payment is recorded. The "Simulate UPI Payment" action described in FR-G-03 is available only in sandbox mode and is labelled as a simulation; it records nothing real.
- **FR-G-05 (P0): Mobile-friendly.** The page works well on a phone browser with no login and no app install.
- **FR-G-06 (P1): Page visibility limits.** Client page never shows other clients' data, vendor names, vendor costs, profit, or internal notes.
- **FR-G-07 (P0): Immediate revocation.** When the owner revokes a link or its expiry passes, access stops immediately. A revoked or expired link shows only "link no longer active", with no project data.

### H. Accounts, Access & Team

- **FR-H-01 (P0): Studio account.** A studio account has one Studio Owner (Admin) and zero or more Team Members.
- **FR-H-02 (P0): Authentication.** One universal sign-in for every user: "Continue with Phone" (SMS one-time password) or "Continue with Email". There are no role-specific login buttons; the workspace and role are resolved after sign-in (FR-H-07).
- **FR-H-03 (P0): Role enforcement.** Access rules in §3.2 are enforced on every screen and every data request, not only in the interface.
- **FR-H-04 (P1): Invitations.** The owner invites team members by phone number or email and assigns the Team Member role. Invitations are matched to a phone number or email at sign-in. Owner role cannot be assigned by invitation.
- **FR-H-05 (P1): Activity log, payment history, and correction trail.** Changes to money records (payments, bills, balances, milestones) are logged with user and timestamp, visible to the owner. Payment history and the correction trail for each record are explicitly viewable.
- **FR-H-06 (P0): Account recovery and session management.** The owner and team members can recover access to their account. Sessions expire after a set period of inactivity, and a user can sign out of all sessions at once.
- **FR-H-07 (P0): Workspace resolution after sign-in.** After sign-in, the system resolves the workspace as follows:
  - A pending invitation matched by phone number (or email) places the user in that studio as a Team Member.
  - An existing studio owner goes to their Owner workspace.
  - A new user completes a 30-second studio setup (studio name, UPI ID, opening cash balance) and becomes Owner of their own workspace on the Free (Starter) tier.
- **FR-H-08 (P0): Free (Starter) tier.** The Free (Starter) tier allows up to 3 active projects and a 30-day forecast horizon. Paid tiers are out of v1 scope.

### I. Onboarding & Setup

- **FR-I-01 (P0): Studio profile.** The owner sets studio name, UPI ID, and contact details (used on the client page and in messages).
- **FR-I-02 (P0): Opening balance.** Required before the forecast is shown.
- **FR-I-03 (P0): Sample data.** A studio can load a seeded demo studio, "Aarav Pixels" (about 5 projects, 15 milestones, 8 vendor bills, and a planned day-14 shortfall), to explore the product before entering real data. Sample data is clearly labelled and removable in one action.
- **FR-I-04 (P1): Guided setup checklist.** A checklist guides a new studio through the setup steps (studio profile, UPI ID, opening balance, first project, first vendor bill) and shows progress.

### J. Public Entry & Navigation

- **FR-J-01 (P0): Public landing page.** A public landing page explains CashPulse and offers "Try the live demo", which opens the seeded studio (FR-I-03) without sign-up.
- **FR-J-02 (P0): Workspace navigation.** The signed-in workspace has these tabs: Cash Runway & What-If, Projects & Contribution, Invoices & Nudges, Vendor Payables, and Settings & Team. A persistent top bar shows the studio name, available cash, the user's role, and a quick "+ New project / invoice" action.

## 7. Business Rules & Calculations

These calculations are deterministic and must be implemented in the system, not produced by AI.

1. **Milestone balance** = milestone amount − sum of payments applied to that milestone.
2. **Project balance** = contract value − sum of all payments received on the project.
3. **Days overdue** = today − milestone due date, when balance > 0 and today > due date. Otherwise 0.
4. **Aging bucket** from days overdue: 0 (not yet due), 1–15, 16–30, 31–60, 61+.
5. **Expected project contribution** = contract value − actual vendor costs; where no actual bill exists for a cost line, use its estimate.
6. **Retention %** = expected project contribution ÷ contract value (shown as "–" when contract value is 0).
7. **Projected balance on day d** = opening balance + Σ remaining inflows dated ≤ d − Σ remaining outflows dated ≤ d, for the forecast horizon. "Remaining" means net of payments already recorded (FR-D-08).
8. **Shortfall** on day d = max(0, safety threshold − projected balance on day d).
9. **Collision candidates.** For a shortfall day, the candidates that could help cover it are:
   - unpaid milestones whose expected date is on or before the shortfall date;
   - overdue milestones with no date, treated as "at risk" candidates;
   - several collections may combine to cover one shortfall;
   - if the candidates cover only part of the shortfall, the remaining gap is shown;
   - collections expected after the related vendor due date are excluded or flagged.
   
   The system lists candidates in order of amount and uses the wording "could help cover this shortfall", never "will resolve".
10. **Currency:** all amounts are in INR (₹), flat and net. Rounding to the nearest rupee for display; calculations keep paise.

## 8. Non-Functional Requirements

- **NFR-01 Usability:** A non-accountant owner can create a project, record a payment, and read the runway without training. Every money term has a plain-language label and tooltip (e.g. "Balance due" rather than "Accounts receivable").
- **NFR-02 Responsiveness and installability:** Web interface works on desktop and mobile browsers (responsive layout). The mobile web app can be installed to the phone home screen and opens full-screen without browser controls. Primary tasks are reachable within two taps on a phone.
- **NFR-03 Performance:** Dashboard, aging, and 90-day runway load within a couple of seconds for a studio with up to 500 projects and 5,000 payments/bills. (Targets to be confirmed in the architecture document.)
- **NFR-04 Data privacy:** Financial data is visible only to the roles in §3.2. Client page exposes only the data in FR-G-02. Client page tokens are not indexed by search engines (no-index). Client-page data is excluded from analytics, logs, and caches.
- **NFR-05 Security:** All traffic over HTTPS. Tokens and credentials are never written to logs. Passwords and tokens are stored securely (method in the architecture document).
- **NFR-06 Auditability:** Every change to a payment, bill, milestone, or opening balance is traceable to a user and time (FR-H-05).
- **NFR-07 Reliability of money math:** Every calculation in §7 is covered by automated tests with worked examples. AI output never feeds these calculations.
- **NFR-08 Availability of core features without AI:** Balances, aging, runway, shortfall flags, templates, and the client page work when the AI assistant is unavailable.
- **NFR-09 Language:** English for v1 UI. Message templates may be written in English with a Hindi or other regional variant added later.
- **NFR-10 Accessibility:** Client page meets basic WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard/screen-reader labels for the stepper and pay button.
- **NFR-11 Studio data isolation (P0):** One studio's data is never visible to, or mixed with, another studio's data, on any screen, request, export, or cache.
- **NFR-12 Recoverable financial records (P0):** Financial records (payments, vendor payments, bills, milestones, and their corrections) can be recovered after accidental change or deletion, and no correction removes the earlier history.

## 9. Conceptual Data Entities

Conceptual only. Field types, storage, and relationships will be defined in the architecture documents.

- **Studio:** name, UPI ID, contact details, safety threshold, default lag for final balance, hold rule setting. Additional fields: upi_vpa, upi_payee_name, owner_phone, city_region.
- **User:** name, email, role (Owner or Team Member), status (invited, active, removed).
- **Team Member:** member_name, member_phone, role_type, invitation_status (extends User).
- **Client:** name, phone, email, notes. Additional fields: whatsapp_phone (mandatory), secondary_phone, city.
- **Project:** client, event dates, contract value, stage, financial status, estimated costs, notes, client-page link status. Additional fields: event_type, venue_location, primary_contact_person, target_album_delivery_date.
- **Milestone:** project, label (Advance / Shoot / Final or custom), percentage, amount, due date.
- **Payment:** milestone (or split across milestones), amount, date, method, reference, status (recorded, voided), audit fields. Refunds are negative payments with a reason. Additional fields: payer_name, payment_mode, transaction_utr_ref, verification_status.
- **Vendor:** name, category, contact, default rate. Additional fields: vendor_phone, payment_handle, linked_project_id.
- **Vendor Bill:** vendor, project (optional), amount, due date, status (unpaid, partially paid, paid), paid date.
- **Vendor Payment:** vendor bill, amount, date, method, reference, audit fields.
- **Recurring Expense:** name, amount, day of month, start and end dates.
- **Opening Balance Entry:** date, amount, entered by.
- **Stage Change:** project, stage, changed by, date, note.
- **Alert:** type, related entity, owner or user target, created date, acknowledged date.
- **Client Link:** project, token, status (active, revoked), expiry date.

## 10. Success Metrics (proposed, to be validated)

- M-01: Share of projects with a complete milestone schedule within 1 day of creation, target ≥ 90%.
- M-02: Share of payments recorded within 24 hours of receipt, target ≥ 80%.
- M-03: Average time from a milestone becoming overdue to a follow-up message being sent, target under 2 days.
- M-04: Owner reports a clearer view of upcoming cash obligations (qualitative, through pilot interviews).
- M-05: Share of client page visits that lead to a UPI payment action, tracked for the pilot.

## 11. Assumptions & Dependencies

- A1. Studios collect payments by UPI or bank transfer and can see the transaction reference and amount in their own app.
- A2. Studios accept that payments are confirmed manually. No automatic bank data is used.
- A3. Studios use WhatsApp to send messages; CashPulse supplies text and links, not a sending channel, in v1.
- A4. Studios host photo galleries on external tools, which CashPulse links to but does not manage.
- A5. Contract values are negotiated as flat net amounts.
- A6. Users have a stable internet connection to use the web app and the client page.
- D1. The UPI deep-link format described in FR-G-03 is supported by common UPI apps.
- D2. Google Gemini (gemini-1.5-flash) is the AI provider, accessed through an AiServiceAdapter, for FR-F-02 and FR-F-03 wording only. The deterministic template fallback applies when it is unavailable (see §12, Q-04).
