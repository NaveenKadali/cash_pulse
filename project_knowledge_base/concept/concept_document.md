# CashPulse: **The Autonomous Back Office & Cash-Flow Co-Pilot.**
---

**Problem Statement:** PS1 – Small Business Operations  
**Target Segment:** Digital-First Independent and Small Scale Photographers  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Framing

Independent wedding and event photographers run creative businesses that are financially fragile. They rarely fail from a lack of customers; they fail because **client money arrives late while subcontracted vendor bills arrive on time**.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                       THE TIMING MISMATCH TRAP                         │
├──────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────┤
│ CLIENT RECEIVABLES (Uncertain)   │ VENDOR PAYABLES (Rigid & Immediate) │
│ • 30% Booking Advance (Fast)     │ • Second Shooters (Due in 48-72h)   │
│ • 40% Shoot Execution (Near)     │ • Drone Pilots (Due on shoot day)   │
│ • 30% Final Delivery (30-90 days │ • Outsource Print Labs (Net-7/COD)  │
│   delays due to photo selection) │ • SaaS Tools & EMIs (Due on 1st)    │
└──────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Digital-First Operating Reality
Modern solo and boutique event photographers operate as **digital-first, asset-light creative directors**:
1. **In-House Core Scope:** Client acquisition, creative direction, on-site photography, curation, color grading, and private cloud gallery delivery.
2. **100% Outsourced Physical Deliverables:** They do not own commercial printing presses or employ bookbinders. Album design, layout spreads, photobook printing, and luxury binding are completely subcontracted to third-party specialist layout designers and commercial print labs (e.g., Canvera, local photo print labs).
3. **Subcontracted Production Crew:** Freelance second shooters, drone pilots, and cinematographers are hired on a per-gig basis.

### The Subcontractor & Milestone Cash Squeeze
* **The Procrastination Gap:** Once the wedding or event ends, clients drag out their photo selections or album spread approvals for 30 to 90+ days. The final 30% milestone balance remains trapped in accounts receivable.
* **The Vendor Clock:** Meanwhile, the photographer's cash outflows cannot wait. Subcontracted crew members demand immediate wages, and commercial print labs demand payment upon order dispatch.
* **The Emotional Barrier:** Photographers hate feeling like aggressive debt collectors. Chasing newlywed couples or families for money over WhatsApp feels awkward and risks damaging word-of-mouth client referrals.


**Core problem:** Lack of consolidated financial visibility across customer collections, outsourced production costs and upcoming business obligations.

## 3. Product Vision

Become the financial co-pilot for digital-first photography businesses, enabling owners to manage customer payments, external vendor obligations and future cash flow with confidence.

CashPulse brings these financial activities together, helping photographers manage payments, track expenses, monitor project-level financial commitments and forecast future cash availability.

**The goal is to help photographers understand not just how much they earn, but how much they actually retain and whether they have sufficient cash to meet upcoming obligations.**

**Important distinction:** CashPulse targets the photography business owner, not album designers, printers or editing agencies.

---

## 4. Proposed Solution

CashPulse provides a unified financial management experience built around six capabilities.

### A. Customer and Payment Management

- Manage customer financial records.
- Create and track invoices.
- Record advances and subsequent payments.
- Monitor pending and overdue customer balances.
- Assist with payment follow-ups.

### B. Outsourced Vendor Expense Management

- Maintain records of editors, album designers and printing vendors.
- Track project-related outsourcing expenses.
- Monitor vendor payments and pending obligations.
- Record expected costs and payment deadlines.

### C. Project-Level Financial Visibility

Understand the financial position of individual photography projects.

Consider:
- Total customer amount.
- Payments received.
- Remaining customer balance.
- Outsourced editing costs.
- Album design costs.
- Printing and production costs.
- Other project expenses.

This helps the photographer understand the expected financial contribution of each project.

### D. Cash-Flow Forecasting

Forecast expected cash inflows and outflows over the next 30–90 days, considering:

- Pending customer collections.
- Expected customer payment dates.
- Upcoming vendor payments.
- Operational expenses.
- Highlights which overdue client invoices are actively starving upcoming vendor payouts.
- Other financial commitments.


### F Assisted Multi-Tone Follow-Ups'
Context-aware, relationship-preserving templates ready to send in 1 click:
* **Tone 1 — Celebratory Sneak-Peek:**
  > *"Hi Priya & Rahul! Loved capturing your reception. Your sneak-peek gallery is now live: [Link]. Please shortlist your top 100 photos so our album designer can begin your layout spreads!"*
* **Tone 2 — Album Layout Approved & Print Release Gate:**
  > *"Hi Priya! The custom album layout is finalized and ready for the print lab. To initiate luxury printing and unlock your high-resolution gallery downloads, please settle the remaining milestone balance of ₹35,000 via [UPI Link]."*
* **Tone 3 — Firm Due Notice (Delivery Freeze):**
  > *"Hi Rahul, checking in regarding invoice #108 (₹35,000), which is now 15 days overdue. Please note that album print dispatch and high-res downloads remain on hold until settled. Please clear via [UPI Link] today."*

### E. AI-Powered Insights and Alerts

Provide understandable financial insights and actionable suggestions.

Examples:

- A customer's final payment is still pending.
- An album printing payment is approaching.
- A project has significant outstanding vendor costs.
- Expected cash availability may become insufficient next month.
- A delayed customer payment could affect upcoming vendor obligations.

* **Actionable Morning Brief:**
  - *"Good morning Aarav: Ping the Verma family today. Collecting their ₹45,000 balance prevents a projected cash crunch before Friday's lab bill."*
